# 战马苦逼打工人 Logo Prompt（DALL·E 3）

## 核心概念

一匹拟人化的战马打工人 —— 既有战马的鬃毛/马头特征，又戴着黑眼圈、坐在电脑前，周围堆满咖啡杯和闹钟。自嘲、有梗、适合作为闹钟 App 的社区形象 / 启动页 Logo。

---

## DALL·E 3 Prompt（直接复制使用）

### Version 1 — App Logo（方形，纯色背景）

> A cute flat vector logo of an anthropomorphic war horse character as a tired office worker (dagongren). The horse has a horse head with mane, wearing a crumpled shirt and tie, sitting at a messy desk. Huge dark circles under its eyes, holding a giant coffee mug in one hoof. On the desk: an alarm clock showing 6:00 AM, scattered energy drink cans labeled "战马", and a stack of papers. Behind it, a window shows dark night sky. Style: clean flat vector, bold outlines, meme-worthy but polished, warm orange and dark blue color palette. White background, circular logo layout. No text. Simple enough for an app icon.

### Version 2 — 横版 Banner / 启动页

> A wide illustration of an anthropomorphic war horse as a tired Chinese office worker. The horse has a horse head with flowing mane, hunched over a desk in a dimly lit room at 2 AM. Multiple empty coffee cups and "战马" energy drink cans scattered around. An alarm clock ringing. The horse's eyes are half-closed with huge dark circles. One hoof typing on a laptop, another holding a coffee IV drip (humorous). Style: digital illustration, warm orange glow from the laptop screen contrasting with cool blue darkness, slight grain texture, cinematic lighting. 16:9 aspect ratio. Humorous but not mean-spirited.

### Version 3 — 极简 App 图标（推荐用于闹钟 App）

> A minimalist app icon of a war horse face with tired eyes and dark circles, wearing a small office tie. One ear droops. Small coffee cup icon in the corner. Background is a gradient from dark blue to warm orange (like sunrise — ironic because the horse never sleeps). Flat vector, rounded corners composition (like iOS app icon), clean bold shapes, no details lost at small size. App icon style.
